Santa Pola

Santa Pola is in the southeast of Spain, on the Mediterranean coast, in the province of Alicante. It is a town of sandy beaches, warm sun for most of the year, fresh seafood, and pink salt flats where flamingos live.

Beaches

Santa Pola has many kilometres of clean sandy beaches with warm, calm water. Playa Lisa is close to the town centre and is a good choice for families, because the sea is shallow and quiet. Gran Playa is long and wide, with plenty of space to walk, play or relax. If you want a more peaceful place, visit Tamarit, a beach with a natural feeling, surrounded by pine trees. From the port, you can also take a boat to the island of Tabarca.

Food

Santa Pola is a fishing town, so the seafood here is very fresh. The town is famous for its prawns, which you can eat grilled or boiled with a little salt. Another local favourite is rice with fish, cooked in a big pan and served hot at the table. Many restaurants near the port and the beach serve fresh fish of the day, and you can enjoy it with a view of the sea.

Water

The Mediterranean sea in Santa Pola is warm and calm for much of the year, so it is perfect for swimming. The clear water around the island of Tabarca is great for snorkelling and diving, and you can also take a relaxing boat trip along the coast.

Sports

If you like action, you can try paddle surf, kayaking, sailing or windsurfing. On land, the beach is a good place for volleyball and football, and the promenade and the paths near the salt flats are great for walking, running and cycling.
